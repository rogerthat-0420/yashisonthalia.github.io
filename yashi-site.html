Who I am

Hi, I'm Yashi.

I'm a therapist who believes that healing doesn't always have to look heavy, serious, or perfectly put together.

Therapy, in my space, is a place to pause. To get curious. To laugh a little (sometimes at ourselves). To notice what your mind has been doing on autopilot and gently invite your body and emotions back into the conversation.

My approach is compassionate, collaborative, and grounded. I don't sit as an expert with answers — we figure things out together. The work is paced, ethical, and deeply respectful of your lived experience.

My Practice

Trained mental health professional with 4 years of experience working with individuals navigating emotional, relational, and psychological concerns.

I hold a Master's in Psychology (Counseling specialization) and am trained in Art Therapy and Couples & Family Therapy.

My practice is guided by Transactional Analysis, narrative ideas, art-based practices, and threads of CBT, DBT, and SFBT — on a client-centered foundation.

Services

🌿

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions for adults to explore their inner world with honesty and support. Collaborative, reflective, and paced with care.

🤝

Couples Therapy

A space to slow down reactive patterns, explore emotional needs, and build healthier ways of relating. Online sessions available.

📚

Supervision

For Master's students and early-career therapists. Focused on case conceptualization, therapeutic presence, ethics, and your growth as a therapist.

🧊

Ice Plunge Workshops

Guided experiences focused on body awareness, courage, and joy — not endurance. Offered as workshops, group sessions, or private bookings.

🌸

Group Spaces

Facilitated group spaces for reflection and shared learning — year diaries, bring-your-own-friend, and group art therapy. Grounded in safety and confidentiality.
